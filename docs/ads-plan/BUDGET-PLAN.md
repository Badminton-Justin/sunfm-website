# Sun FM — Budget Plan

**Starting monthly budget:** $1,200/month ($40/day)
**Scaling target by month 6:** $2,500-3,500/month
**Spend cap (Phase 1):** $1,500/month — do NOT exceed without 2 weeks of CAC under $150

## Pacing Strategy (Month 1)

| Week | Daily Budget | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Week 1 | $25/day total | Soft launch. Watch conversion firing daily. Pause anything broken. |
| Week 2 | $35/day total | Ramp if Week 1 looked clean. |
| Week 3 | $40/day total | Full budget. Start optimization decisions. |
| Week 4 | $40/day total | First real performance review. |

**Do NOT scale daily budget mid-month** even if performance looks great. Google's auto-bidding goes back into "learning phase" with budget changes >20%. Wait until end of Month 1 to ramp.

---

## Month 2-3 Budget Evolution

### If CAC is on target (<$150 per consultation)
- Increase total budget to $1,500-1,800/month
- Shift to 70/20/10 split:
  - 70% to whichever campaign is Proven (likely Non-Brand — Location)
  - 20% to Promising (likely Non-Brand — Intent)
  - 10% to Testing (Meta retargeting + Bing)
- Switch winning Search campaigns from Maximize Conversions → Target CPA

### If CAC is too high (>$200 per consultation)
- Do NOT raise budget
- Tighten:
  - Cut underperforming keywords (3x Kill Rule: spend >$300 with zero conversions = pause)
  - Tighten geo radius
  - Adjust device bid (often: cut tablet, optimize mobile)
  - Revisit landing page (run /ads-landing audit)

## Maximum Stop-Loss Rules

**Hard pauses (no overrides):**
- Any single ad group spending >$300 with zero conversions → pause immediately
- Total monthly spend reaches $1,500 in Month 1 → pause all campaigns regardless of performance (verify tracking before unpausing)
- CAC exceeds $300 for 4 consecutive weeks → pause non-brand campaigns, keep brand only

**Soft pulls (review before continuing):**
- Single keyword spending >$50 with zero conversions in 7 days → pause that keyword
- Ad group CTR below 2% for 14 days → revise ad copy
- Click-to-form-start rate on /start below 40% → audit landing page

## Bid Strategy Decision Matrix

| Campaign | Conversions in last 30d | Recommended Strategy | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Brand Defense | Any | Maximize Conversions | Cheap clicks, simple strategy |
| Non-Brand Location | 0-15 | Maximize Conversions | Let Google find conversions |
| Non-Brand Location | 15-29 | Maximize Conversions w/ tCPA target | Soft hint to Google |
| Non-Brand Location | 30+ | Target CPA at $100-130 | Mature, predictable |
| Non-Brand Intent | 0-30 | Maximize Conversions | Lower volume; need more data first |
| Non-Brand Intent | 30+ | Target CPA at $120-150 | Slightly higher tolerance |

**Never use:** Target Impression Share, Manual CPC (unless brand campaign), Maximize Clicks (wastes budget on non-converting clicks).

## Budget Tracking

Weekly cadence in Google Ads tab of the SEO tracking sheet (using `/ads-analytics 2026-MM-DD`):
- Spend
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Conversions
- Cost per conversion (calc'd)
- Conv rate (calc'd)

Monthly review questions:
1. Are we on pace for our monthly budget? (no spike, no underspend)
2. Is CAC trending down month over month?
3. Which campaigns are Proven / Promising / Testing right now?
4. Should we shift 10-20% of budget from one tier to another?

Quarterly review questions:
1. Is total ad spend trending up alongside consultation volume? (good)
2. Or is spend up but consultations flat? (bad — investigate)
3. What's the realized customer lifetime value of paid-acquired clients vs. organic-acquired? (informs target CAC adjustment)

## Math Reference

### Target unit economics
- **Target CAC (Cost per booked consultation):** $100-150
- **Consultation → Paid client rate:** estimated 30-40% (track in Sheets)
- **Effective cost per paid client:** $250-500
- **Average client revenue (estimated):** $200-400/month × 6-month retention = $1,200-2,400 LTV
- **LTV:CAC ratio target:** 3:1 minimum, 5:1 healthy, 8:1+ excellent

### Break-even thresholds
- Cost per consultation can go up to **$300** if conversion → paid client rate is 50%+
- Cost per consultation must stay under **$120** if conversion → paid client rate drops below 25%
- Watch this ratio monthly via the consultation tracking sheet
