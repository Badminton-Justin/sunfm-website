# Sun FM — Creative Brief

**Phase 1 Creative Scope:** Google Search ads only (Responsive Search Ads). No display, video, or static images required for launch.

---

## Responsive Search Ad Anatomy

Each ad group gets **1-3 RSAs**. Each RSA can have:
- Up to **15 headlines** (30 chars each)
- Up to **4 descriptions** (90 chars each)
- 2 path fields (15 chars each)

Google rotates and selects combinations. The job is to give Google strong raw material.

---

## Brand DNA (run via /ads-dna to refine)

**Tone:** Plainspoken, confident, movement-positive, anti-hype
**Audience filter:** Busy professionals 30+
**Value props (in order):** Mobility, strength for longevity, undoing desk-job damage, real personal attention, long-term clients
**Trust signals:** ACE-certified, 12,000+ sessions, 107 reviews, founded 2020
**Forbidden:** "Transform," "shred," "guaranteed results," "secret," "boot camp"

## What to NOT Write

Per Sun FM voice rules (verified throughout the conversation):

❌ **"Transform your body in 90 days"** — overpromising
❌ **"Crush your fitness goals"** — bro tone
❌ **"Guaranteed results"** — legal + trust risk
❌ **"Boot camp"** — wrong positioning
❌ **"99% of my clients..."** — fabricated stats forbidden
❌ **"Better than [competitor]"** — movement-positive memory
❌ **"Secret to..."** — clickbait
❌ **"Limited time offer"** — fake urgency
❌ **"Get ripped"** / **"Get shredded"** — bodybuilder vocabulary

---

## A/B Test Plan (Phase 2)

Once 2-3 ad groups have 50+ clicks each, start RSA-level A/B tests:

**Test 1:** Outcome vs. Pain-point opener
- Variant A: Lead headlines with outcomes ("Train for Longevity")
- Variant B: Lead with pain-points ("Tight Hips? Bad Back?")

**Test 2:** Studio location vs. service area
- Variant A: Emphasize San Jose studio
- Variant B: Emphasize South Bay service coverage

**Test 3:** Free consultation vs. free Movement Screen as primary CTA
- Variant A: "Book Free Consultation"
- Variant B: "Take Free Movement Screen First"

Use PostHog for downstream conversion attribution (which variant led to actual form submits).
