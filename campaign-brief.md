## Brand DNA Summary
Sun FM is a 1:1 personal training studio at 1401 Parkmoor Ave in San Jose, founded by Jeffrey Sun (Stanford Human Biology, former EMT, ACE-certified) and built for busy professionals 30 to 60 who sit too much and want to train without breaking down. The voice is plainspoken, opinionated, and credible — first person from Jeff, never hype, anchored in 12,000+ logged sessions and 107 five-star reviews. Visual identity is warm beige backgrounds, charcoal text, and a sparing red accent (#CB4538), with imagery that reads gym-real and candid rather than stock or bodybuilder.

## Campaign Concepts

### Concept 1: Free Home Workout Set
**Hypothesis:** A concrete, time-bound offer paired with the free consultation gives cold searchers a low-friction reason to convert this week instead of bookmarking the page. Tangible gear beats abstract promises for a price-conscious 30-60 audience.
**Primary Message:** Book a free consultation through June 30 and I'll send you home with a workout set so you can start moving the same week.
**Tone:** Direct, generous, no-pressure. First person from Jeff, plainspoken about what's included.
**Visual Direction:** Jeff at the studio handing a client a small bundle of training tools (mini-band, resistance band, foam roller). Warm window light, beige wall behind. No stock smiles. Candid mid-conversation moment.
**Target ad groups:** AG_PT_San_Jose, AG_PT_Sunnyvale, AG_PT_Santa_Clara, AG_PT_Cupertino, AG_PT_Mountain_View, AG_PT_Other_Cities
**CTA:** Book my free consultation
**Addresses (audit finding):** Reinforces high-scoring trust signals and leads with the active launch offer (Free home workout set through June 30) per the skill's lead-with-offer rule for Concept 1.

### Concept 2: Fix What Sitting Did To You
**Hypothesis:** Desk-worker pain is the silent search intent across the South Bay tech corridor, and no competitor names it directly — leading with the problem ("tight hips, sore back, locked shoulders") earns the click from people who've already tried generic gyms and quit.
**Primary Message:** I'll find what's actually causing your back, hip, or shoulder pain and build a program that fixes the root, not the symptom.
**Tone:** Calm, diagnostic, problem-aware. First person, no scolding, no movement-comparison framing.
**Visual Direction:** Close-up of Jeff coaching a client through a hip mobility drill on the studio floor. Client mid-30s in workout clothes, focused expression, not posing. Charcoal mat, beige wall, single overhead light source.
**Target ad groups:** AG_Desk_Worker_Fix
**CTA:** Request my free movement screen
**Addresses (audit finding):** Closes Gap 3 (desk-job rehab is wide open) and Gap 1 (mobility-first positioning unclaimed). Strengthens Non-Brand Intent message match where landing page currently scores 65/100.

### Concept 3: Train For The Long Run
**Hypothesis:** The over-30 audience has been pitched 12-week sprints for two decades and is skeptical. "Train so you can keep training in 10 years" is the durable promise nobody else in the South Bay is making, and it sorts for the right client (long retention, high LTV).
**Primary Message:** I coach mobility and strength so your training holds up for decades, not for a six-week sprint.
**Tone:** Confident, durable, anti-hype. First person. Frames longevity as the goal without claiming superiority over any other method.
**Visual Direction:** Wide studio shot of a longtime client (40s-50s) mid-deadlift, Jeff cueing from the side. Both look settled, in-work, not performative. Warm late-afternoon light, beige and charcoal palette, no neon, no extreme physiques.
**Target ad groups:** AG_Over_40, AG_Return_to_Training
**CTA:** Book my free consultation
**Addresses (audit finding):** Closes Gap 2 (longevity framing unclaimed) and reinforces the credibility moat (12,000+ sessions, multi-year clients) that aggregator landing pages cannot match.

### Concept 4: Built For Busy Pros
**Hypothesis:** Tech and healthcare professionals in their 30s-50s want done-for-them training expertise so their limited gym time actually moves them forward, instead of being spent guessing what to do.
**Primary Message:** I build the plan and coach every session so your time at the studio is spent training, not figuring out what to train.
**Tone:** Respectful of time, expert-led, results-focused. First person, no boot-camp energy.
**Visual Direction:** Mid-day studio coaching moment. Jeff guiding a focused mid-30s client through a goblet squat. Warm beige walls, natural side light, calm. No rah-rah aesthetics, no performative cueing. Documentary feel.
**Target ad groups:** AG_Busy_Professionals
**CTA:** Request my free consultation
**Addresses (audit finding):** Replaces the Concept-3 fallback for the busy-professional intent ad group with a precision-and-time-respect framing that converts better for that specific psychographic.

### Concept 5: Real Mobility Coaching
**Hypothesis:** Searchers using mobility-specific terms (hip mobility, posture, range of motion) want technical credibility from someone who reads bodies, not generic stretching content or yoga aesthetics.
**Primary Message:** I find what's actually tight, weak, or compensating, then we build the work around it.
**Tone:** Technical credibility, body-aware, root-cause focused. First person, calm, no movement-method comparisons.
**Visual Direction:** Hands-on hip mobility assessment. Jeff kneeling beside client on a low padded platform, guiding their leg through internal rotation. Clinical-but-warm lighting. Side angle. Studio background slightly blurred.
**Target ad groups:** AG_Mobility
**CTA:** Request my free movement screen
**Addresses (audit finding):** Replaces the Concept-2/3 fallback for the mobility-specific intent ad group with a technical specialist framing more aligned to high-intent mobility searches.
